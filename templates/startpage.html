Why should you know?

Knowing whether you are an introvert or an extrovert is important because it allows you to manage your energy levels and choose environments that help you thrive rather than lead to burnout. Knowing this will help you:

  • Optimize your Social Battery
  • Better Career Alignment
  • Improve Relationships

About the model

Dataset

The model is trained with a dataset by Shalma Muji. This has 8 columns and 5000 rows of data.

Preprocessing

The data provided is preprocessed using Label Encoding and Feature Scaling

Classifier

The model is built using the Logistic Regression Classifier
